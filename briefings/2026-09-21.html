🔐 Custody & wallet infrastructure

Direct competitor moves, MPC/HSM tech, qualified-custodian licensing, key management.

Bastion wins conditional OCC approval for a national trust bank charter

What:Bastion Platforms National Trust Company will offer stablecoin custody, wallets, payment infrastructure and white-label issuance out of a single federally regulated entity.
Source:Cointelegraph
So what: This is the clearest version yet of the bundle we compete against: custody plus issuance plus rails under one charter. Worth pulling the conditional approval letter to see which activities the OCC fenced off, since those conditions are effectively a map of what a trust charter cannot do for us.

SEC crypto custody rewrite enters White House review

What:The planned rule covers investment advisers and investment companies and is meant to clarify digital-asset custody. It follows the withdrawal of the separate 2023 safeguarding proposal.
Source:The Defiant
So what: The single most consequential item on this page for us. Whatever definition of qualified custodian and "possession or control" survives OMB review becomes our RIA sales script and our attestation scope. Start drafting the comment letter before the text drops so we are not writing it in a two-week scramble.

CFTC extends its Phantom wallet relief to the whole industry

What:Staff opened introducing-broker relief to software developers generally, giving every non-custodial wallet and DeFi interface the same no-action posture it previously gave Phantom.
Source:Bankless
So what: Lowers the regulatory cost of shipping a self-custody or MPC wallet that routes to third-party venues, which is exactly the surface a treasury client asks us for. Relief is staff-level and revocable, so scope any build to survive its withdrawal.

Banks now make up 23% of the EU MiCA provider register

What:Banks roughly doubled their presence on ESMA's MiCA register since late June and now account for nearly one in four authorized providers.
Source:Cointelegraph
So what: In Europe the incumbent banks are now the competition, not the channel. If our EU go-to-market still assumes banks need us to reach crypto, that assumption is six months stale. The sub-custody and white-label pitch is the one that still works.

Lagarde reportedly intervened to block Binance's EU MiCA license

What:The WSJ reported the ECB president stepped into Binance's Greek MiCA application. Binance called the reports speculation and said it remains committed to European authorization.
Source:CoinDesk · Cointelegraph
So what: MiCA passporting is supposed to be a technical process, and this says it is partly political. If we are picking a home-state regulator, weight ECB relations alongside processing time, and do not assume the fast jurisdiction is the safe one.

🏦 Treasury, RWA & corporate adoption

SEC clears tokenized stocks to trade onchain under a five-year exemption

What:The conditional exemption lets tokenized NMS stock trade on permissioned automated market makers with full shareholder rights attached, including third-party-issued tokens. CFTC staff widened software relief the same day.
Source:The Defiant · Ledger Insights
So what: Full rights is the phrase that creates work for us. Holding a rights-bearing token means handling dividends, splits, proxy votes and tax lots, none of which our current token model represents. Analysts already name Coinbase, Robinhood and Circle as the early winners, so the window to be the neutral custodian for everyone else is narrow.

Morpho opens USDC borrowing against Coinbase's tokenized stocks

What:Holders of five Coinbase stock tokens can pledge them for USDC loans on Base.
Source:The Defiant
So what: A treasury client can now borrow dollars against equity exposure without leaving the chain, which is a genuinely useful working-capital tool. Our risk engine has no equity collateral model, no market-hours logic and no halt handling. That is the gap to scope.

Why post-quantum cryptography looks different for tokenized finance

What:OpenZeppelin argues that treating quantum risk as a distant standards-body problem is too narrow, because the lattice and hash-based replacements have different performance and key-size characteristics than the elliptic-curve signatures onchain finance is built on.
Source:OpenZeppelin
So what: The most useful read on this page for anyone touching our key management. Larger signatures and different verification costs affect HSM capacity, MPC round complexity and transaction fees, so crypto-agility belongs in the signing architecture now rather than in a migration project later. Enterprise RFPs are already asking.

North Korean fake recruiters infected 30,000 devices and took $10.7M

What:The WaterPlum group targeted developers with fake jobs at crypto, AI and NFT companies, reaching more than 30,000 devices across over 100 countries.
Source:Cointelegraph
So what: This is an insider-threat vector aimed straight at engineers with commit access and signing credentials. Our controls should assume a developer laptop is compromised: hardware-backed signing, no long-lived credentials on endpoints, and a review gate on anything that touches the signing path.
