Source:CoinTelegraph
So what: A Bitcoin-native, HSM-backed offering from a well-known name is a new competitor for BTC-heavy treasuries. Know where our HSM and air-gap story beats or trails it before it shows up in an RFP.

Unocoin keeps deposits and withdrawals halted over suspect transfers

What:Unocoin said the transfers were signed and broadcast outside BitGo's platform. BitGo said its account freeze worked as intended.
Source:CoinTelegraph
So what: This is the case where a client holds key material that can sign around the custodian. Be ready to explain who can sign outside our policy engine, and what our freeze and alerting does when that happens.

North Korea stole $400M using deepfake video calls, DOJ says

What:The FBI says the FaceStealer scheme uses prerecorded footage and AI deepfakes of trusted contacts to get crypto workers to install malware on video calls.
Source:Decrypt
So what: Approvers on client teams are the target. Out-of-band approval checks, hardware-bound approver devices and a 'never install on a call' rule should be in onboarding material and security docs.

Luxor hit by an AI-enabled attack that redirected client hashrate

What:Attackers redirected mining power through Luxor's Commander platform. Luxor says it patched the flaw within two hours, no data left the system, and it will compensate the dozen or so affected accounts.
Source:The Block
So what: A management console with no keys still moved value. Audit our own admin and config surfaces (payout addresses, whitelist edits) with the same rigor as the signing path.

Trezor cuts its reseller network by 80% after finding more spy implants

What:Trezor brought in stricter supply-chain controls and dropped most resellers after finding tampered devices.
Source:Protos
So what: If clients use hardware devices as approvers, we need a sourcing and attestation policy for them. Device provenance is a quick win for our security questionnaire answers.

Source:The Block

What:Draft rules would also require stablecoins to move only between accounts or wallets belonging to the same person.
Source:The Block
So what: Same-owner rules need proof of wallet ownership and per-jurisdiction velocity limits. Both are policy-engine features we can generalize instead of building one-offs for each country.

AI agents get wallets: OpenAI on cards, Coinbase with server-side limits

What:OpenAI's ChatGPT wallet lets agents buy using Visa, Mastercard and Amex, with no crypto involved. Coinbase added Agent Controls that cap trade size, limit 24-hour volume and stop an agent after a portfolio drop, all enforced on its servers.
Source:Decrypt / Bankless
So what: Agent spending is going to card rails first. For us, the useful lesson is Coinbase's model: limits enforced on our side, not the agent's. That maps directly onto API-key policies for automated treasury flows.
