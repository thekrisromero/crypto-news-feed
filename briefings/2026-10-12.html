🔐 Custody & Wallet Infrastructure

Ledger confirms hardware implant in reseller-sold devices; losses may exceed $86M

What:Ledger says all confirmed draining cases involve devices sold by CryptoBilis, a reseller in Southeast Asia, and at least one affected device contained an unauthorized hardware implant. Ledger's direct sales channels are unaffected. CryptoBilis has paused all hardware wallet sales, and Ledger reminded resellers never to restock returned products.
Source:CoinTelegraph · The Defiant · CoinDesk
So what: Clients will ask whether this touches us. Prepare a short note on where our signing hardware comes from (vendor-direct, tamper-evident chain of custody), and add device-sourcing questions to the security questionnaire we send clients who self-custody alongside us.

DWF Labs subsidiaries sue BitGo for $141M over alleged token lock-up breach

What:Two DWF Labs subsidiaries filed suit against BitGo for $141M, alleging BitGo breached a token lock-up arrangement tied to early token sales.
Source:CoinDesk
So what: Lock-ups and vesting schedules enforced by a custodian are a liability surface, not just a feature. If we offer time-locked or restricted balances, check that the contract terms, the policy rules and the audit trail all say the same thing.

Source:CoinTelegraph
