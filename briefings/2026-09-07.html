🔐 Custody & Wallet Infrastructure

Direct competitor moves, MPC/HSM tech, qualified-custodian licensing, key-management.

SEC crypto custody rewrite enters White House review

What:The planned rule covers investment advisers and investment companies and clarifies digital-asset custody, following the withdrawal of the separate 2023 safeguarding proposal.
Source:The Defiant
So what: OMB review means text is weeks, not quarters, away. This is the moment to inventory which of our controls map to a plausible "qualified custodian" test — segregation, insolvency remoteness, independent verification — and flag any gap as a roadmap P0 rather than discovering it during a client's compliance review.

Liquid sidechain paused after 3,998 BTC left the federation wallet

What:Blockstream disabled Liquid's bridge nodes after almost the entire bitcoin reserve backing L-BTC — roughly $320M — was withdrawn to a single address whose owner wrote "we are whitehats" into a transaction. Exchanges suspended L-BTC deposits and withdrawals.
Source:The Block · The Defiant
So what: A functioning federation multisig still allowed a full sweep to one destination — the failure was policy, not cryptography. Worth auditing whether our withdrawal policy engine can express "no single destination above X% of AUC in one window" and whether we alert on destination-concentration, not just amount.

Tether-backed Orionx shuts down after audit flags $7M custody gap

What:The Latin American exchange is closing permanently after an audit found more than $7 million in customer assets had moved to wallets outside its custody.
Source:CoinTelegraph
So what: Segregation failures are still being caught by annual audit rather than continuous reconciliation. A continuously-attested customer-liability-vs-onchain-assets view is the clearest product story we can tell against exchange-run custody — and a strong candidate for a customer-visible dashboard, not just an internal control.

Trezor breach widens by 67,000 customers; Ledger sued for $500M over its own breaches

What:Trezor disclosed that its shipping partner ShipMonk retained records from 2019–2021 — names, emails, phone numbers, shipping addresses — well beyond the 90-day retention its partners had agreed to. Separately, Ledger faces a $500M suit over a string of data breaches.
Source:Protos · Decrypt
So what: Both incidents are third-party data-retention failures, not key compromises — which is exactly the risk shape our own vendor chain carries. Worth a concrete ask to security this quarter: enumerate every subprocessor holding client identity or shipping data and verify retention is enforced technically, not contractually.

G7 warns quantum threat demands action; QuFi ships post-quantum verification on Bitcoin testnet

What:The G7 urged organizations to adopt post-quantum security before capable computers can compromise current encryption and digital signatures. QuFi launched a platform that verifies digital-asset transactions with post-quantum cryptography without requiring changes to existing settlement networks.
Source:Decrypt · CoinTelegraph
So what: Institutional RFPs will start asking for a written PQC migration position within two or three quarters. A one-page statement — which key material is at risk, what our rotation path looks like, where we depend on HSM vendor roadmaps — is cheap now and expensive to improvise later.

💰 Stablecoins & Payments

Issuance, reserves, bank rails, cross-border settlement, freeze and seizure mechanics.

21 financial institutions commit to a joint dollar stablecoin venture

What:Bank of America, Citi, Goldman Sachs, UBS, Deutsche Bank and sixteen other firms said they will incorporate a company in the second half of 2026 to issue a dollar stablecoin, with market launch to follow.
Source:The Defiant
So what: A bank consortium coin will arrive with counterparties our institutional clients already face, which makes it a default rather than an option. If our asset-onboarding path is a manual engineering task per issuer, that's the constraint to fix before the venture incorporates — not after it launches.

Revolut starts EURR rollout with Bridge as regulated issuer

What:Revolut opened EURR to selected customers in three countries, using Bridge as the regulated issuer. Reported outstanding supply is tiny against Circle's EURC, which sits near €394.5 million.
Source:The Defiant
So what: The distributor-plus-licensed-issuer split is becoming the standard European structure. Our reserve-attestation and issuer-risk model needs to represent two distinct entities per token — who redeems and who is regulated are no longer the same answer.

Wyoming puts state stablecoin reserves onchain with Chainlink Proof of Reserve

What:The Wyoming Stable Token Commission adopted Chainlink Proof of Reserve for the Frontier Stable Token on Sept 2. FRNT supply stands at 967,948 tokens, roughly 0.2% of the 508.7 million the Commission has projected.
Source:The Defiant
So what: A US state issuer choosing machine-readable proof of reserve sets the reference standard clients will point at. Consuming PoR feeds as a first-class signal in our reserve-health view — rather than PDF attestations — is a small build with a disproportionate trust story.

Tether sued over $42.4M frozen three months before a seizure warrant

What:Two Thai businessmen are suing Tether in Manhattan federal court over 42,417,785.62 USDT blacklisted across ten Ethereum addresses. Onchain records show all ten were frozen inside a single two-and-a-half-minute window, months ahead of the warrant.
Source:The Defiant
So what: Issuer freeze authority is now being litigated as a standalone risk, separate from reserve quality. Clients holding operating balances in a single stablecoin need us to surface freeze exposure explicitly — per-issuer concentration in the treasury dashboard, with an automated rebalance suggestion.

Bank of Korea study: dollar stablecoins can push local currencies lower

What:A Bank of Korea study finds that dollar-backed stablecoin adoption exerts downward pressure on local currencies, arriving as Korea builds its tokenized securities framework.
Source:CoinDesk
So what: Expect non-USD jurisdictions to push local-currency stablecoins for domestic settlement. Multi-currency treasury support is a market-access requirement in APAC and EMEA, not a nice-to-have for later.

🏦 Treasury, RWA & Corporate Adoption

Tokenized securities, corporate balance sheets, ETF flows, digital-asset treasury companies.

South Korea to tokenize "all types" of securities in three stages from 2027

What:Korea's financial regulator published a three-phase roadmap, targeting February 2027 for the country's first tokenized securities framework, and ultimately aims to let participants settle tokenized securities onchain using stablecoins.
Source:The Block
So what: The stablecoin settlement leg is the detail that matters for us: it makes custody of the cash leg and the security leg a single product problem. Worth a roadmap placeholder for atomic DvP support against a regulated Korean venue with an 18-month lead time.

London Stock Exchange to support xStocks tokenized trading in Payward deal

What:Payward, Kraken's parent, is partnering with the LSE to bring UK equities onchain, issuing tokens for the 100 largest London-listed companies in the coming weeks.
Source:Ledger Insights
So what: An incumbent exchange lending its name to tokenized equities changes the diligence conversation from "is this legitimate" to "who holds it." That is a custody question, and we should have a clear answer on corporate actions and dividend handling for tokenized equities before clients ask.

AMC vs Robinhood turns into an industry fight over which tokenized-stock model wins

What:AMC's CEO called Robinhood's stock tokens unregulated and put outside securities counsel on it; Robinhood's chief legal officer told him to send lawyers. Uniswap's Hayden Adams compared Robinhood's and Ondo's tokens to early stablecoins, while Dinari's co-founder called the same instruments materially worse. AMC shares jumped 21% overnight.

Our asset-listing policy should require issuer-level backing disclosure before any tokenized equity is supported, or we inherit someone else's legal argument.

Digital-asset treasury companies keep levering up: HyperStrat to $2.5B, Strategy resumes buying

⚖️ Regulation & Policy

Revolut and a16z-backed OpenReserve win preliminary OCC national bank approvals

What:OpenReserve Holdings received preliminary OCC approval for a national bank focused on onchain settlement after a $25 million seed, choosing a full charter over the trust-bank route most crypto firms take. Revolut still needs FDIC, Federal Reserve and final OCC clearances before a planned 2027 launch.
Source:The Block · Decrypt
So what: Full national charters bring insured deposits and lending alongside stablecoin issuance — a bundle a pure custodian cannot match. Our competitive positioning should lean harder on independence and multi-venue neutrality, since we will increasingly be compared against a bank rather than a wallet.

Source:The Block · The Defiant

What:The National Sheriffs' Association dropped its consumer-protection opposition to the CLARITY Act and will let the legislative process proceed. ⚙️ Smart Contracts & Infrastructure

Protocol changes, chain reliability, account abstraction, privacy primitives.

EIP-7906 would let Ethereum accounts inspect and veto a transaction's actual result

What:The proposal would let accounts check what a transaction actually did and, if the outcome is malicious, reject it — a post-execution condition check rather than a pre-signature guess.
Source:Bankless
So what: This is the most directly relevant protocol item of the week for us: it turns policy enforcement we currently do in our own simulation layer into a chain-native guarantee. Worth an engineering spike on what our policy engine could delegate if this ships, and where our simulation stays differentiated.

Ethereum blob usage hits an all-time high; Robinhood Chain data went missing for 14 minutes

What:Rollups pushed Ethereum blob demand to a record. In the same window, Robinhood Chain kept producing blocks every 101 milliseconds but its transaction data failed to reach Ethereum for exactly 14 minutes across two gaps; Arbitrum pointed at Ethereum's blob market. Robinhood Chain gas fees also rose 82-fold in eleven days, topping $4.45 million in a single day.
Source:The Defiant · Bankless
So what: Blob contention is now a live L2 availability risk, and a chain that looks healthy at the sequencer can be unfinalized underneath. Our confirmation policy for L2 assets should key off data availability on L1, not sequencer block height — and blob-market congestion belongs on the ops dashboard.

World open-sources ProveKit for private identity proofs

What:World released ProveKit as open source, letting users prove identity claims such as age or nationality without exposing the underlying personal data.
Source:Bankless
So what: Set against this week's two data-retention breaches, selective-disclosure proofs are the structural answer to holding less client PII in the first place. Early to build on, but the right frame for how we talk about identity in our next compliance-roadmap review.
