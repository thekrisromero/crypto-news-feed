SEC Proposes Scrapping Two NMS Rules That Block Tokenized Stock Trading via DeFi AMMs

What:The SEC proposed on June 11 to rescind Rule 611 (the "trade-through rule" requiring best-execution routing) and Rule 610(e) from Regulation NMS, opening a 60-day comment period. The rollback would let tokenized stocks trade through DeFi AMMs without best-execution constraints — a major unlock for on-chain equity markets.
Source:Ledger Insights
So what: This is the biggest regulatory unlock for tokenized equity trading yet. If finalized, it removes a structural barrier that has kept tokenized stocks in a legal gray zone. File a comment, brief your legal team, and begin roadmapping what qualified custodian support for tokenized equities looks like under the new framework.

Japan's Lower House Passes Crypto Securities Bill — Path to ETFs and 20% Tax Rate

What:Japan's lower house passed a bill amending the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act to regulate crypto like stocks — opening a path to regulated crypto ETFs by 2027 and cutting capital-gains tax from up to 55% to a flat 20%. Upper house passage is still pending.
Source:The Defiant
So what: Japan is one of the world's largest retail crypto markets. Custody platforms with Japan exposure or clients operating there need to understand how the FIEA classification changes their regulated-entity obligations — Japan ETF custody could be a significant new revenue line if you're already licensed there.
