🔐 Custody & wallet infrastructure

Competitor moves, qualified-custodian rules, staking operations and the incidents that test them.

SEC proposes custody rules for advisers and funds

What:The proposal would let registered advisers and funds use state-chartered trust companies as custodians, and would permit self-custody under certain conditions. Comments are due 60 days after it is published in the Federal Register.
Source:The Block
So what: If our adviser and fund clients can self-custody, the SEC will want evidence of controls. Read the self-custody conditions line by line against our policy engine, audit logs and segregation reports. Some of those could move from nice-to-have to required. We should also decide whether to file a comment.

Anchorage Digital reportedly cuts 17% of staff

What:The $4.2B crypto bank is cutting about 17% of its workforce while pushing into stablecoin issuance and taking a $100M investment from Tether.
Source:CoinTelegraph
So what: A direct competitor is narrowing its focus toward issuance. Expect institutional prospects to ask harder questions about vendor runway, so have our continuity and exit-plan documentation ready for RFPs.

MetaMask unstakes $1.4B of ETH after validator rewards are stolen

What:MetaMask disclosed a security incident in its staking operations in which validator rewards were stolen, then unstaked about $1.4B of ETH. It said user wallets faced no immediate threat.
Source:Protos
So what: Fee-recipient and withdrawal addresses are an attack surface separate from client keys. Check that changing them on a staked position goes through the same approval policy as an outbound transfer.

Chainalysis ties the $387M Bitget hack to North Korea

What:Chainalysis says the Sept. 24 breach pushed North Korea-linked thefts in 2026 past $1B. It used in-house AI tools to trace the funds across four chains, and stolen XRP was swapped through THORChain.
Source:The Defiant
So what: The counterparty is now a sanctioned state actor. Confirm our deposit screening flags Bitget-hack addresses, including funds that have passed through THORChain.

NEAR Intents loses $3.8M, then gets all of it back

What:A deposit/withdrawal exploit drained $3.8M on Oct. 1 and disrupted deposits and withdrawals on 11 networks. The team patched it, named the attacker, set a 48-hour deadline, and received the funds back in full (about 34.59 BTC).
Source:The Block
So what: Intent and solver networks are becoming where withdrawals actually settle. If we route anything through one, we need outage monitoring and a fallback rail.

💵 Stablecoins & payments

Issuer economics, bank settlement pilots and the EU reserve fight.

Circle asks the EU to drop MiCA's bank-deposit mandate

What:In its MiCA review filing, Circle asked the European Commission to replace mandatory bank-deposit minimums with more flexible liquidity rules, keep cross-border co-issuance, and create a recognition route for foreign stablecoins. Central banks are asking for stronger safeguards.
Source:The Defiant
So what: The result decides whether USDC issued outside the EU stays usable for our EU clients. Keep stablecoin eligibility configurable per issuer and jurisdiction, not hardcoded.

Stripe's Bridge launches OUSD, a stablecoin that pays its distributors

What:OUSD is live on four chains and shares issuer economics with the platforms that distribute it.
Source:Bankless
So what: We hold client stablecoin balances, so we may qualify as a distributor. Worth a call with Bridge to learn the terms, and with legal on what client disclosure it would need.

Visa: business programs drive about 17% of stablecoin card volume

What:Payment volume on Visa's stablecoin-linked cards grew nearly 200% year over year in its fiscal second quarter. Business programs account for roughly 17% of it.
Source:The Defiant
So what: Corporate spend from stablecoin balances is real now. Treasury clients will ask for cards funded from custody balances, so a card-issuer integration belongs on the discovery list.

Lloyds and Visa settle $750,000 in USDC

What:Lloyds settled $750,000 of payment obligations with Visa in USDC during a seven-day live cross-border pilot.
Source:The Block
So what: A UK clearing bank settling real obligations in USDC is our treasury use case. Whatever reporting they needed (FX reference rates, settlement confirmations, GL reconciliation) should be in our requirements too.

Fiserv launches the bank-only Roughrider coin on Solana

What:VersaBank issues the token. Fiserv says it serves a North Dakota network of more than 90 banks and credit unions.
Source:The Defiant
So what: Permissioned tokens on public chains are a new asset type for us. Our allowlist model needs to handle tokens that only verified bank wallets can hold or receive.

🏦 Treasury, RWA & corporate adoption

Tokenized equities, fund records onchain and treasury-company stress.

OKX and NYSE parent ICE file for 24/7 tokenized US stock trading

What:The joint venture wants to offer tokenized shares in more than 60 US-listed companies under the SEC's new innovation exemption. Robinhood's crypto chief said the same exemption still limits how fast stock tokens can come to the US.
Source:CoinTelegraph
So what: Tokenized equities that trade around the clock break our assumption of a market close. List what depends on it: valuation snapshots, corporate actions, reporting cut-offs.

Brazil's central securities depository mirrors BTG fund shares on the XRP Ledger

What:CSD BR is putting BTG Pactual fund share records on the public XRP Ledger with Ripple. CSD BR's own records stay the legal source of truth.
Source:Ledger Insights
So what: When the token is a mirror, the custodian has to show both records and reconcile them. A per-asset 'legal source' field and a reconciliation status would cover this.

Strategy has spent 64% of its STRC rescue fund

What:After 10 weeks of buybacks, Strategy has used 64% of its $2B STRC fund and has less than $724M left. Separately, crypto treasury companies are struggling to keep their valuation premiums.
Source:Protos
So what: Treasury companies under pressure may sell or pledge BTC. For that segment, collateral and fast-unwind workflows will matter more than long-term cold storage.

⚖️ Regulation & policy

Charter fights, custody independence, state tax and sanctions.

Community banks sue the OCC over crypto trust charters

What:The ICBA complaint targets Protego's conditional approval and asks the court to stop the OCC from granting national trust charters under its disputed rule and guidance. The group calls the charters a 'side door into the banking system.'
Source:The Defiant
So what: Several competitors are planning around a national trust charter. If the suit succeeds, state trust charters (which the SEC proposal accepts) gain value. Track which partners depend on OCC charters.

BitGo CEO says combining exchange and custody is a risk 'potentially worse than Lehman'

What:Mike Belshe told The Block that Clarity's failure left systemic risk from single firms that run exchange, brokerage and custody together. Bitwise's Matt Hougan takes the other side: Clarity's death gave crypto faster SEC wins.
Source:The Block
So what: Independence from trading venues is a selling point for a standalone custodian, but only with proof: segregation, no rehypothecation, separate legal entity. Put those in the sales deck.

Illinois agrees to delay its crypto tax by six months

What:The delay still needs court approval. The levy would charge customers on asset value, not gains, and the draft rules cover paid custody, transfers and stablecoin settlement.
Source:The Defiant
So what: A tax on value held in paid custody hits our fee lines for Illinois clients. Ask legal whether we would be the collecting party, and check whether billing can carry a per-jurisdiction tax line.

US designates Russia's A7 network a transnational criminal organization

What:Treasury's proposed rule would cut A7's front companies off from US finance, and FinCEN proposed banning sub-agent payments. FinCEN says A7 sub-agents processed more than $17B in dollar transactions from January 2025 to June 2026; its ruble token moved $179B. Japan also added Garantex to its sanctions list.
Source:The Defiant
So what: Update screening lists for A7-linked entities and its ruble token, and confirm our vendor has the Japan Garantex listing.

Source:The Defiant
So what: L2 withdrawal timing can change in the middle of an incident. Arbitrum withdrawal ETAs should come from live chain status, and ops should get an alert when a chain declares an emergency.

OpenZeppelin and T-REX rebuild ONCHAINID as a smart account

Source:OpenZeppelin
