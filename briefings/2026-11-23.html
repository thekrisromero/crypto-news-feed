🔐 Custody & Wallet Infrastructure

Direct competitor moves, MPC/HSM tech, qualified-custodian licensing, key management.

Chainflip losses reach $12M after suspected key compromise

What:Chainflip disclosed that 122.9 BTC was stolen on top of earlier ETH outflows, bringing losses to about $12M. The Block reports the apparently automated attack drained vaults through a suspected key compromise; Chainflip offered a 10% bounty with a Nov 23 deadline. FLIP fell about 34%.
Source:The Block
So what: Threshold vaults are only as strong as their signer ops. Use this as a reference case in sales conversations, and confirm our key ceremony and signer rotation runbooks can be shown to prospects on request.

Ledger tampered devices drain $1M; CTO to step down

What:Ledger users who bought devices from Dubai Virgin outlets and German resellers lost about $1M in suspected spyware incidents, and more tampered units were reported in Berlin and the UAE. CTO Charles Guillemet will leave at the end of 2026.
Source:Protos
So what: Clients who self-custody part of their treasury on hardware wallets are exposed to supply-chain risk. Onboarding guidance on device sourcing and attestation, plus a co-signing option through our platform, would close that gap.

Lighter pauses withdrawals after a leaked API key drain

What:The perp exchange said a trader's leaked API key caused the drain, not a contract bug, and paused withdrawals.
Source:Bankless
So what: Review our API key model: per-key scopes, IP allowlists, withdrawal permissions off by default and destination allowlists. If any of these are optional today, consider making them defaults.

Fluidkey: unpatched tool let attacker stage $1.6M in transfers

What:Fluidkey's post-mortem says stolen session tokens let an attacker stage transfers that users' own apps then signed. Personal data and viewing keys were also exposed.
Source:The Defiant
So what: Signing a transaction someone else staged is the failure mode. Clear-signing screens and out-of-band approval for any transfer not created by the approving user should be in the policy engine backlog.

VanEck drops Gemini as ETF custodian, keeps Coinbase

What:VanEck will end Gemini's custody agreements for three crypto ETFs in February 2027. Coinbase stays on as custodian.
Source:CoinTelegraph
So what: Issuers are consolidating custodians, which puts more concentration on Coinbase. Some issuers may want a second qualified custodian, which is an opening if our licensing supports it.

Bitcoin's quantum problem reaches bridges and custodians

What:CoinDesk examines how a quantum threat to Bitcoin signatures would reach bridges, custodians and coins with exposed public keys. Separately, SHRINCS is emerging as a front-runner post-quantum signature for Bitcoin, and Vitalik backed leanPCS (draft EIP-8288) for cheaper post-quantum signatures on Ethereum.
Source:CoinDesk
So what: Institutional due-diligence questionnaires will start asking about post-quantum plans. Draft a short statement covering address reuse, exposed-pubkey inventory and how we would migrate keys, even if the timeline is years out.
